# lemontabs privacy policy

_Last updated: November 24, 2026_

lemontabs is a Chrome extension that replaces the new tab page with an overview of your open tabs.

## What it reads

- **Open tabs and tab groups** (titles, addresses, icons, group names and colors), to display them, switch to them, close, move, sort and group them when you ask.
- **Bookmarks**, only to show matches while you type in the search box.
- **Site icons**, from Chrome's local icon cache.

## What it stores

A few preferences (theme, which windows to show, dismissed hints and confirmations, and the "keep same-site tabs together" switch). They live only in your browser's extension storage.

## What it sends

Nothing. lemontabs has no server, no analytics, no ads and no third-party code. Your tabs, bookmarks and preferences never leave your device and are not sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

The only network request the extension makes on your behalf is opening `google.com/search` when you press Enter in the search box, which is the same as typing the search into Google yourself.
